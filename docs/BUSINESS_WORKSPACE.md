# Business workspace integration

/app/business/:id validates an active membership before opening the complete existing Business shell. Customers/orders/invoices, payments/reconciliation, Stage 2 intelligence/operations, Stage 3 Passport/credentials/proofs, team and settings remain available according to existing permissions.

Business setup creates organization, owner membership, typed workspace and profile preferences transactionally. Setup is not business verification, provider approval or live financial activation. Existing organization IDs, data, roles, legacy consent boundaries and signed history are preserved.

The Business organization list excludes Personal/Developer resource containers. All workspaces links back to the account switcher. Existing invitations can add an individual with any primary experience to a Business membership; no employee-owned company is required. Last-owner safeguards remain.

Existing live integration, distributed operation, external authorization and legal launch gates still apply. See Stage 1–3 runbooks and PRODUCT_FUNCTIONAL_AUDIT.
