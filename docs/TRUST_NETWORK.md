# Trust Evidence Network
Owner: AfriCore product/engineering. Reviewed 2026-10-26.

Evidence references existing organization-scoped profile, provider event, invoice, order, shipment or relationship records. Each retains source identifier/hash, purpose, asserted lawful basis, reporter method, assurance, observation time, limitations and retention window. Imported or API-shaped data is not automatically authoritative. Sandbox provider events remain simulations. Current evidence is rechecked before issuance; changed/expired sources require refreshed authorization.

A business requests a named counterparty relationship. Only a different authorized counterparty can confirm/reject; a member of the requesting business cannot independently confirm its own organization. Either party can revoke a confirmed relationship. This prevents straightforward self-endorsement but does not prove independence or eliminate collusion/Sybil accounts. No unvalidated fraud accusation or automated penalty is produced.

Evidence Coverage Index v1 is deliberately a coverage indicator: profile, payment, order, shipment and relationship categories each carry20%. Present active evidence yields that category's20%; absent data yields no coverage contribution, not a negative judgment. Source-verified counts remain separately visible. Snapshot records preserve version/inputs. This is not a loan-default probability, credit score, profitability estimate or guarantee of behavior. Do not use it for financing approval. AFriCore Capital remains deferred.

Initial abuse controls include membership checks, source scoping/hash dedupe, approved issuer types, signature/status checks, no levels2/3 without real sources, self-confirmation denial and correction review. Independent identity deduplication, collusion graph analysis and live source integrations remain open. External institutional authority cannot be fabricated to make the demo appear stronger.
