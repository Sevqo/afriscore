# Educational content

AfriCore Learn (/learn) contains 16 curated articles across Payments, Business Finance, Operations, Trust and Developers. Topics cover payment links, reconciliation, invoices, cash flow, receivables, business records, intelligence, automation, protected payments, Business Passport, credentials, blockchain, privacy, developer APIs, webhooks and sandbox operation. Twelve linked definitions live at /learn/glossary.

Articles contain a definition, practical explanation, why it matters, labelled fictional example, annotated process, steps, common mistakes, current limits and related product/article links. The library has local text search, category filtering and an explicit no-results state. Seven fictional audience scenarios cover a merchant, SME, contractor, supplier, growing team, Passport holder and developer.

Four local simulations demonstrate reference matching/partial allocation, receipt timing, selective disclosure and separate credential checks. They do not call payment, settlement, credential issuance or sharing APIs. Workspace reconciliation and Passport details provide compact contextual help.

Primary conceptual references were checked on 2026-10-26: IFRS IAS 7 (cash-flow concepts), W3C Verifiable Credentials Overview (credential roles and verification), and KRA eTIMS (distinguishing statutory invoicing from this sandbox). These references do not establish AfriCore certification, integration or legal compliance.
