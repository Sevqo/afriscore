# Fulfillment and evidence
Orders, payment and delivery have independent states. Shipment line quantities use item_index+quantity and cannot exceed remaining non-returned/non-cancelled quantities. Multiple shipments are supported. Seller transitions follow pending -> processing -> ready_for_dispatch -> shipped -> out_for_delivery -> delivered, with exception/return/cancellation branches. Backend validates transitions; order summaries derive from shipments. Buyer full receipt checks shipped quantity coverage and records customer_confirmed assurance.

Seller-entered courier/reference/milestones are seller_reported. Sandbox connector tracking is sandbox_simulated and never promoted to provider-verified delivery. No live courier callback, OTP identity check, geolocation attestation or signature verification exists. Buyer-link possession is not independent identity assurance.

Evidence is append-only through services, scoped to organization+environment+resource. Statements may have TXT/PDF/PNG/JPEG attachments up to 250000 bytes. Magic bytes and encoding are checked, SHA-256 recorded, file contents encrypted through the platform seal helper; authenticated downloads force attachment/octet-stream and audit access. Metadata remains database text. No active content preview is performed.

The access window is 365 days. Expired evidence is excluded from APIs. Automatic physical purge, legal holds, malware scanning, object-storage lifecycle/backups and privacy deletion workflows remain deployment work. Do not claim full retention enforcement or malware-free uploads. Protect encryption keys and backups separately; production must fail closed without an encryption key. Buyer evidence access is restricted to its own dispute. Logs redact buyer/checkout bearer tokens.
