# Dispute management
A buyer capability opens one unresolved dispute per protected agreement in eligible paid/inspection states. Reasons cover non-delivery, incorrect/damaged/missing items, delivery disagreement, payment discrepancy and contract issues. Inspection deadlines are enforced; exceptional late cases require support rather than an invented automatic refund.

Opening records the buyer statement/evidence and marks the agreement disputed. Sellers append a response/evidence during a 14-day review window. Evidence and histories retain their source and never establish truth by themselves. An authorized admin records a reasoned release/refund decision only with an unexpired approval requested by that reviewer and granted by another owner/admin. Cross-tenant/buyer-link access is denied. No self-approval is allowed.

Resolution marks the dispute resolved and requests a partner outcome; it does not mark funds settled. Explicit sandbox partner confirmation records released/refunded. The simulator cannot settle while another unresolved dispute exists. Notifications are in-app only. Cases and financial command results are auditable and idempotent.

This is merchant sandbox governance, not independent arbitration. Independent platform case officers, appeal handling, evidence legal hold, partial settlements, external mediation, service-level escalation and production dispute policy remain launch gates. Customer statements and seller milestones cannot be advertised as verified courier proof.
