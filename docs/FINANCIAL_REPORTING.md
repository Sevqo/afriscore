# Financial reporting refinement

Business report CSV uses the same applied dates/currency as the visible report. The shared date component validates ISO dates and rejects reversed ranges. Reset clears the applied range. Personal summaries include sparse, actual manually recorded income/expense/net daily series; no artificial observations are inserted. Integer minor units and independent currency buckets remain authoritative.

Obligations can be edited, paused, resumed, completed or cancelled. Only active obligations enter forecasts; historical records remain. Recorded net movement is not profit or a bank balance. Outstanding receivables have their explicitly documented coverage rather than implying a date-filtered bank total.
