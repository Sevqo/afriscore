# Content strategy and inventory

Reviewed 2026-10-26. This continuation implements both the illustration/chart brief and the companion content specification, including the three supplied screenshots.

Baseline audit: the public pages shared short generic descriptions and repeated trust bullets; the workspace mockup did not explain a transaction; the editorial figures reused awkward anatomy; charts had no point inspection or range controls; green top rules decorated financial cards. Existing business APIs, permissions and financial semantics remain authoritative.

The delivered content map has 23 typed product/audience guides, 16 learning articles, 12 glossary definitions and seven fictional business scenarios. Core products, intelligence/automation, commerce, trust, Connect/Data, developer onboarding and sandbox each have a specific purpose, audience, problem, workflow, example, result, limits, FAQs and related destinations. Company/legal notices remain separate and do not acquire invented policies or partnerships.

Homepage order: clear purpose and a labelled invoice/receipt/remainder example; product-family choices; business problem; connected records; individual tools; audience stories; security; interactive matching; protection education; getting started; availability. Detailed product learning is progressively disclosed in workflows, examples and FAQs rather than repeated marketing paragraphs.

Navigation adds AfriCore Learn and the glossary. Product demo CTAs select the relevant existing workspace section without resetting demo records. Developer examples stay separate from merchant explanations. Learning-center search filters the curated local library; it does not send queries to a service.

Canonical content sources: website/src/education/guides.ts and Learn.tsx. Reusable presentations: Components.tsx and DeveloperTutorial.tsx. Build-time HTML, titles, descriptions, Open Graph text and canonical links are generated by website/scripts/prerender.mjs. Set AFRICORE_SITE_URL to the deployed origin to produce absolute build-time canonicals and a sitemap; local builds use relative canonicals. No public deployment domain is invented.

See PRODUCT_EXPLANATIONS.md, EDUCATIONAL_CONTENT.md, CONTENT_ACCURACY.md and CONTENT_QA.md for scope and verification.
