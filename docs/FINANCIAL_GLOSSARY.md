# Financial glossary maintenance

The user-facing canonical definitions are in website/src/education/Learn.tsx and rendered at /learn/glossary. Keep their plain-language explanations and contextual article links together rather than duplicating a second editable dictionary here.

Core distinctions: a payment request asks for money; a confirmed receipt has trusted event provenance; an allocation links that receipt to an obligation; reconciliation explains the relationship and exceptions. Receivables are amounts customers owe. Cash movement records inflows less outflows for the selected period; profit also depends on accounting recognition and is not derived from that difference alone. A forecast is an assumption-based scenario.

A verifiable credential is a signed claim. Signature validity, current status, issuer recognition and supporting evidence are separate checks. A blockchain anchor can support integrity/history checks, not the truth of the underlying business statement. Selective disclosure shares selected claims with a named recipient; it is not a promise that all information is public or verified.
