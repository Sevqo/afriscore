# Product
Owner: AfriCore engineering. Reviewed: 2026-10-25.

AfriCore connects business operations and programmable financial infrastructure, starting in Kenya. Business and Developers share organization-owned services. KES and Africa/Nairobi are initial display defaults; every financial record has explicit currency.

Public pages: Home, Business, Pay, Invoicing, Reconciliation, Cash Flow, Connect, Data, Developers, Documentation, Sandbox, Social Commerce, SMEs, Contractors/Suppliers, Fintechs/SaaS, Security, About, Contact, Support, Pricing, Privacy and Terms. Unpublished pricing/legal/support information is labeled honestly.

The operational app offers Overview, Payments, Orders, Invoices, Customers, Cash Flow, Transactions, Reconciliation, Reports, Integrations, Developers, Team and Settings. Metrics use persisted records in the selected organization/environment. Marketing product artwork is separately labeled illustrative sandbox data.

Phase 1/2 is sandbox-focused. Financing, advanced intelligence and protected payments remain future capabilities. See IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md for acceptance gaps before conducting any real-data pilot.
